# Slice S3: Sell request and arrival

Status: not started

## Goal
The story from "I want to sell my car" to "Received" works with three accounts: a customer sends a sell request, the manager of the chosen branch accepts or rejects it, and sales staff record the arrival of the car.

## Use cases
UC-12 Submit Sell-Your-Car Request, UC-13 Track Requests and Bookings, UC-23 Review Sell Request, UC-14 Record Vehicle Arrival.

## Requirements (word for word from the SRDS)
- FR-CU-11 Sell-your-car request. The system shall let a logged-in customer submit a sell
  request with branch, make, model, year, mileage, expected price and description. The request
  shall start in the Pending status. [Phase 1; UC-12]
- FR-CU-12 Track requests and bookings. The system shall let a customer view the status and
  history of their sell requests, inquiries, test drive bookings and inspection bookings.
  Records shall be kept after completion. [Phase 1; UC-13]
- FR-BM-01 Sell request decision. The system shall let a Branch Manager accept or reject a
  Pending sell request of their branch. A rejection shall have a reason. An accepted request
  shall expire if the seller does not arrive in time. [Phase 1; UC-23, UC-39]
- FR-SS-01 Record vehicle arrival. The system shall let Sales Staff record the arrival of a
  vehicle linked to an accepted sell request, or as a walk-in vehicle without a request. The
  vehicle shall be created in the Received status and the request shall become Completed.
  [Phase 1; UC-14]
- FR-SS-08 Branch work list. The system shall show Sales Staff the accepted sell requests,
  their vehicles and their inspections of their own branch, with filters by status. [Phase 1;
  UC-14]
- FR-AC-01 Lifecycle statuses. The system shall move every vehicle through the statuses
  Received, Under Inspection, Reconditioning, Ready for Sale, Booked, Sold and Delivered. An
  additional status Rejected shall be used for vehicles refused after inspection (assumption
  A-02). [Phase 1; UC-14, UC-24]
- FR-AC-02 Allowed transitions. The system shall allow only the transitions shown in the state
  model in Section 5.5 and shall refuse any other change. [Phase 1]
- FR-AC-03 Status history. The system shall store every vehicle status change with the old
  status, new status, user, time and note, and shall show the history to staff and managers.
  [Phase 1; UC-19]
- FR-AC-05 Unique vehicle identity. The system shall not allow two non-deleted vehicles with
  the same registration number or the same chassis number. [Phase 1; UC-14]
- FR-AU-07 Branch restriction. The system shall limit Sales Staff and Branch Managers to the
  records of their own branch for sell requests, inspections and vehicle management. Inventory
  viewing across branches is allowed for Branch Managers (FR-IV-01). [Phase 1; UC-14, UC-23,
  UC-24]
- FR-NT-02 Email notifications. The system shall send emails for the events marked as email in
  the Notification Events table in Section 3.1.12. A failure to send an email shall be logged
  and shall not stop the user action. [Phase 1; UC-04]
- A-03 Sell request statuses are Pending, Accepted, Rejected, Expired and Completed. An accepted request expires if the seller does not arrive within 7 days.
- A-05 The customer chooses the branch for a sell request or an inspection booking, and the manager of that branch handles it.

## Rules for this slice
- A new request is Pending and the branch manager is notified (in-app and e-mail). Accept or reject notifies the customer (in-app and e-mail).
- Accepting sets expires_at to 7 days later (A-03, LIMITS). A rejection needs a reason.
- Recording an arrival is refused when expires_at has passed, even if the hourly job (S8) has not run yet.
- An arrival is one transaction: create the vehicle with status received, complete the request, write the first history row. If the vehicle insert fails, nothing is saved.
- A walk-in arrival uses the same form without a request (FR-SS-01).
- Registration number and chassis number must be unique among non-deleted vehicles. Show a friendly message for a duplicate.
- Every status change goes through changeStatus and transitions.js. The sell request statuses are in packages/shared.
- Branch rule: staff and managers see only their own branch. Staff of another branch get 403.

## Data
Migration: sell_requests, vehicles, vehicle_photos, vehicle_status_history (and vehicle_documents, table only, Proposal). Constraints as in docs/data-model.md. Indexes: sell_requests (branch_id, status), (customer_id), and (status, expires_at) where accepted.

## API
GET /api/branches (done in S1). POST /api/sell-requests. GET /api/sell-requests/mine. GET /api/sell-requests?status=. GET /api/sell-requests/:id. POST /api/sell-requests/:id/accept. POST /api/sell-requests/:id/reject. POST /api/vehicles/arrivals.

## Screens
- Sell your car (customer): one column. Branch first, then the car, then the expected price and a description. After sending, go to My requests and show "Sell request sent".
- My requests (customer): a plain list with status badges. A rejected request shows the reason under the row.
- Sell requests (staff and manager): tabs Pending, Accepted, All. Managers see Accept and Reject (reason dialog). Sales staff see Record arrival on accepted rows.
- Arrival form: prefilled from the request; registration number shown as a plate; a walk-in version with empty fields.

## Not in this slice
Photos on the vehicle (S5), inspections (S4), the expiry job (S8), the Expired badge text (S8), booking and sale.

## Done when
1. A customer sends a request; the manager of that branch sees it, accepts it and the customer is notified.
2. Staff open the accepted request, record the arrival, and the vehicle is Received and the request is Completed.
3. A walk-in arrival works without a request.
4. A duplicate registration number gives a friendly message and saves nothing.
5. The manager of another branch cannot see or decide the request.

## Tests that must exist (AT-10 to AT-16, AT-40 partly)
- Normal: customer sends; manager of that branch accepts; staff record arrival; vehicle Received; request Completed. Walk-in arrival.
- Refusals: staff cannot send a request. Another branch cannot see or decide it. Rejection without a reason. Arrival for a request that is not accepted. Arrival after expires_at. Duplicate registration or chassis number. A forced failure in the last step leaves no history row.
- Notifications: the manager gets one when a request is sent; the customer gets one on accept and on reject.
