## Goal
The manager reviews a submitted inspection and approves it with acquisition terms and a selling price, returns it with remarks, or rejects the vehicle. Staff manage the listing. Every status change shows in the history.

## Use cases
UC-24 Review Inspection Report, UC-25 Set Acquisition Terms and Price, UC-18 Manage Vehicle Listing and Documents, UC-19 View Vehicle History.

## Requirements (word for word from the SRDS)
- FR-BM-02 Inspection review. The system shall let a Branch Manager open a submitted
  inspection report and approve it, return it with remarks, or reject the vehicle with a
  reason. [Phase 1; UC-24]
- FR-BM-03 Acquisition terms. On approval the system shall require the manager to choose
  Purchase with a purchase price, or Consignment with a commission percentage, and to enter
  the selling price. [Phase 1; UC-25]
- FR-AC-04 Acquisition record. The system shall store for every accepted vehicle whether it
  was acquired by purchase or consignment, with the purchase price or the commission
  percentage, and who approved it. [Phase 1; UC-25]
- FR-SS-03 Edit listing. The system shall let Sales Staff edit the description, specification
  details and photos of a vehicle listing of their branch. Price fields shall not be editable
  by Sales Staff. [Phase 1; UC-18]
- FR-SS-04 Delete listing. The system shall let Sales Staff delete a vehicle listing by soft
  delete. A deleted listing shall not be visible to the public and the deletion shall be
  recorded in the vehicle history. [Phase 1; UC-18]
- FR-AC-03 Status history. The system shall store every vehicle status change with the old
  status, new status, user, time and note, and shall show the history to staff and managers.
  [Phase 1; UC-19]
- A-20 The Branch Manager decides at approval whether a vehicle needs reconditioning.
- A-02 Reconditioning can be skipped when no repair is needed.

## Rules for this slice
- Approve is one transaction: save the acquisition (purchase price, or consignment with a commission percent), set the selling price, change the vehicle status, write the history and the audit rows, send the notifications.
- The manager answers "needs repairs": no gives ready_for_sale (listed), yes gives reconditioning (not listed, waits for S6). The inspection becomes approved.
- Return needs remarks (the inspection becomes returned and the vehicle stays under_inspection). Reject needs a reason (the vehicle becomes rejected).
- The selling price must be above zero. A purchase needs a purchase price. A consignment needs a commission percent.
- Sales staff can edit description, specification and photos of vehicles of their branch, but never the price (FR-SS-03). Soft delete with a history row (FR-SS-04).
- Only the manager of the branch decides. Another branch gets 403. Lock the inspection row in the decision transaction.
- Notifications: approved, returned or rejected goes to the inspector (in-app only). Vehicle listed goes to the seller (in-app and e-mail), when the car becomes Ready for Sale (Proposal).

## Screens
- Manager review: the whole report on the left, the decision panel on the right. Only the fields of the chosen acquisition type are shown. A box "Needs repairs before sale" beside the price; when ticked the main button says "Approve and send to repairs". Return and reject open a reason dialog.
- Vehicles (staff): a table with plate badge, car, status badge, price and actions; edit form; history panel; a Reconditioning filter.

## Not in this slice
Repair costs (S6), the public pages (S7), the price change approval (Later, UC-30), vehicle documents screens.

## Done when
1. After an approval without repairs the vehicle is Ready for Sale and the history shows every step from arrival to listing.
2. After an approval with repairs the vehicle is in Reconditioning and is not in any public list.
3. Return and reject work with their remarks and reasons and the inspector is notified.
4. Staff cannot change the price. A soft-deleted vehicle disappears from the staff list default view and leaves a history row.

## Tests that must exist (AT-21 to AT-23, AT-28, AT-29, AT-30)
- Normal: approve with purchase and price; approve with consignment and commission; approved vehicle is Ready for Sale and the history lists who and when; approve with needs repairs gives Reconditioning.
- Refusals: sales staff cannot approve. A price in a staff edit is refused. Approve without a selling price or with zero. Return or reject without remarks. Another branch's manager. A forced failure in the last step leaves no half-approved vehicle.
