## Goal
A car approved with "needs repairs" is invisible to the public, collects repair costs, and becomes Ready for Sale only when the manager marks the work complete.

## Use cases
UC-27 Manage Reconditioning and Repair Costs. The SRDS marks it Later; the plan moves it into the 35% (Proposal).

## Requirements (word for word from the SRDS)
- FR-AC-06 Reconditioning. When repair work is needed, the system shall hold the vehicle in
  the Reconditioning status until the manager marks the work complete, after which the vehicle
  becomes Ready for Sale. [SRDS marks it Later; the plan moves it in; UC-27]
- FR-AC-07 Repair cost records. The system shall let the Branch Manager record repair and
  reconditioning expenses for a vehicle with description, vendor, amount and date. [SRDS marks
  it Later; the plan moves it in; UC-27]
- A-02 Reconditioning can be skipped when no repair is needed.
- A-20 The Branch Manager decides at approval whether a vehicle needs reconditioning.

## Rules for this slice
- Only the manager of the vehicle's branch may list, add and complete. Sales staff and customers get 403. Another branch's manager gets 403.
- A repair item needs a description, a vendor, an amount above zero and a date. Adding is allowed only while the vehicle is in reconditioning.
- The total is the sum of the items. Money stays text from the database and is added by the database or by a safe helper, never by floating point arithmetic in JavaScript.
- Complete is one transaction: set the vehicle to ready_for_sale through changeStatus, write the history row, notify the seller that the car is listed.
- If no item is saved, complete answers 409 until the body says confirmNoWork true.
- Only the total of repair costs is shown. The full acquisition cost (FR-AC-08) is not built.

## Data
Migration: repair_costs with a check that the amount is above zero and an index on vehicle_id.

## API
GET /api/vehicles/:id/repairs. POST /api/vehicles/:id/repairs. POST /api/vehicles/:id/repairs/complete.

## Screens
- Repairs panel (opens from a vehicle in Reconditioning): a small form on top (description, vendor, amount, date), the items below, the total in bold under the list, the button "Add repair cost", and "Mark work complete" at the bottom.
- With no item saved, a dialog asks "No repair cost is saved. Was any work needed?" with the choices "No work was needed" and "Go back".
- A Reconditioning filter on the staff vehicle list.

## Not in this slice
Repair approvals, vendors list, profit calculation, documents, the stock list (S8).

## Done when
1. A car approved with needs repairs does not appear in the public list.
2. Two repair items show with the correct total.
3. After "Mark work complete" the car is Ready for Sale, appears in the public list (once S7 exists, or in the staff list now), and the seller is notified.
4. Complete with no item asks for confirmation.

## Tests that must exist (AT-31 to AT-34)
- Normal: add two items; the total is their sum; complete; the vehicle is Ready for Sale. Complete with no item after confirming that no work was needed.
- Refusals: an amount of zero or less. An item for a vehicle not in Reconditioning. Complete with no item and no confirmation (409). Sales staff (403). A manager of another branch (403).
