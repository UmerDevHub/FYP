# Slice S7: Public pages

Status: not started
Size: 5 days (Estimate). Server: Umer. Screens: Habib (Proposal). Habib may start earlier with seeded vehicles, because these screens only read data.
Read first: docs/progress.md, docs/srds-35.md (section 8), docs/api-contract.md (S7), docs/design.md.

## Goal
A person with no account can find a car that is Ready for Sale, open its page and read its inspection report, on a phone-sized screen.

## Use cases
UC-05 Browse and View Vehicles, UC-06 View Inspection Report.

## Requirements (word for word from the SRDS)
- FR-CU-01 Browse vehicle listings. The system shall show all vehicles in the Ready for Sale
  status, which are not deleted, to visitors and customers. Each card shall show the cover
  photo, make, model, year, price, mileage and branch. [Phase 1; UC-05]
- FR-CU-02 Search and filter. The system shall filter vehicles by keyword, make, model,
  variant, year range, price range, mileage range, fuel type, transmission, body type, engine
  capacity, colour, city and branch. Selected filters shall be kept in the page address.
  [Phase 1; UC-05]
- FR-CU-03 Sort and paging. The system shall sort the list by newest, price, year and mileage
  and shall show results in pages. [Phase 1; UC-05]
- FR-CU-04 Vehicle details. The system shall show photos, specifications, price, description
  and branch of a vehicle. Internal data such as acquisition price, commission and manager
  remarks shall never be shown. [Phase 1; UC-05]
- FR-CU-05 View approved inspection report. The system shall show the approved inspection
  report of a listed vehicle, including checklist results, photos, mileage and observations.
  [Phase 1; UC-06]
- FR-IN-07 Inspection report. The system shall generate a structured inspection report from
  the submitted data, showing vehicle details, inspector, dates, checklist results,
  observations and photos. The report shall be printable. [Phase 1; UC-06, UC-16]
- NFR-PF-01 Public pages (browse and vehicle detail) should be usable within 3 seconds on a
  normal broadband connection with a typical vehicle list.
- NFR-PF-02 Common read requests (list, detail, filters) should answer within 1 second and
  write requests within 2 seconds under normal load, not counting file upload and AI calls.
- NFR-US-02 The public pages and the customer pages shall work on phone screens from 360
  pixels width. Staff pages shall work on a desktop screen and on a tablet.
- NFR-SC-08 Personal data of customers (name, phone, email, requests) shall be visible only to
  the customer and to staff who need it for their work. Public pages shall never show personal
  data or internal cost data.

## Rules for this slice
- Only vehicles that are Ready for Sale and not deleted appear. A vehicle in any other state, or deleted, gives 404 on its detail address.
- The report shows only approved inspections.
- Public answers never contain the acquisition price, the commission, the acquisition type, the manager's remarks, password hashes or tokens. Public queries select an explicit list of columns, never everything.
- Proposal: registration and chassis numbers are not shown until question Q7 is answered.
- Proposal: start with five filters (make, model, year range, price range, fuel). Add the other filters of FR-CU-02 one at a time later.
- Chosen filters, sorting and the page number are kept in the page address, so a link can be shared (FR-CU-02). Sorting: newest, price low to high, price high to low, mileage low to high; add year sorting to match FR-CU-03.
- Paging: limit at most 50. A bad filter value gives 400 with a field error.
- Mobile first. The report is part of the car page and has a print style (no header, no filters, black text, page break between categories).
- The cover photo must arrive in the list (a known past bug: test it).

## Data
No new table. Index: vehicles (status, listed_at desc) where not deleted.

## API
GET /api/vehicles. GET /api/vehicles/:id. GET /api/vehicles/:id/inspection-report.

## Screens
- Car list: cards with cover photo, make, model, year, price tag on the photo (bottom left), mileage and branch as one sentence ("74,000 km, petrol, automatic"). Filters on the left on wide screens and above the list on a phone. No-result state.
- Car detail: gallery, specs, price tag, then the inspection report (a one-line summary first, notes about damage, then the checks by category; each result is a word with an icon). A short sentence for a car without an approved report.

## Not in this slice
Favourites, comparison (not built, A-10), chatbot, inquiry, test drive booking, fair price check.

## Done when
1. A visitor finds a Ready for Sale car with filters, opens it and reads the report at 360 px width.
2. Filters survive a reload and a shared link.
3. The raw JSON of every public answer has none of the private fields.
4. The printed report is clean.

## Tests that must exist (AT-24 to AT-27)
- Normal: only Ready for Sale and not deleted vehicles appear; filters and sorting give the right order; the report shows only approved inspections.
- Refusals: Received, Booked, Sold, Reconditioning and deleted vehicles do not appear and give 404 on detail. The answer text never contains purchase price, commission, acquisition type or manager remarks.

## Open points
