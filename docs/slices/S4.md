## Goal
Sales staff can inspect a received vehicle with the 24-item checklist, notes, mileage and photos, save a draft as often as they like, and submit it. The manager is notified.

## Use cases
UC-15 Conduct Vehicle Inspection. (UC-16 Generate Inspection Report is finished in S5 and S7.)

## Requirements (word for word from the SRDS)
- FR-IN-01 Start inspection. The system shall allow Sales Staff to start an inspection for a
  vehicle in the Received status of their branch. The vehicle shall move to Under Inspection.
  [Phase 1; UC-15]
- FR-IN-02 Standard checklist. The system shall show a standard checklist grouped in
  categories. For every item the inspector shall select Good, Fair, Poor or Not Applicable and
  may add a remark. [Phase 1; UC-15, UC-35]
- FR-IN-03 Mileage and documents. The system shall record the mileage found at inspection and
  whether the vehicle documents were verified, with notes. [Phase 1; UC-15]
- FR-IN-04 Damage and repaint notes. The system shall record visible damage and repaint
  observations as text notes. [Phase 1; UC-15]
- FR-IN-05 Inspection photos. The system shall let the inspector upload photos with captions.
  Only JPG and PNG files up to 5 MB shall be accepted. [Phase 1; UC-15]
- FR-IN-06 Draft and submit. The system shall save an inspection as a draft and shall submit
  it only when every checklist item has a result, mileage is entered and the minimum number of
  photos is attached. [Phase 1; UC-15]
- FR-IN-08 Inspection history. The system shall keep every submission and every manager
  decision of an inspection, including returned remarks, so that the full history can be seen.
  [Phase 1; UC-15, UC-24]
- A-06 Photos and documents are stored on the server disk through a storage service. At least 4 inspection photos are needed to submit an inspection.
- NFR-SC-07 Uploaded files shall be checked for size and real file type, renamed with random
  names and stored outside the program code folder.
- NFR-PF-03 Uploading one photo of up to 5 MB should complete within 15 seconds on a
  connection with an upload speed of at least 3 Mbps, and the user shall see upload progress.

## Rules for this slice
- Start an inspection only for a received vehicle of the staff member's own branch. The vehicle becomes under_inspection. Only one open inspection per vehicle (a partial unique index plus a check in the service).
- Checklist: 24 items in 6 categories (Proposal, kept in the seed file). Answers are Good, Fair, Poor or Not applicable. A remark is asked for Fair and Poor.
- Submit needs: every item answered, mileage entered, at least 4 photos (A-06). If something is missing the answer lists exactly what.
- Save draft always works, even when the form is incomplete. Nothing can be edited after submit, except after a return.
- Photos: JPG and PNG only, at most 5 MB, checked by the first bytes of the file, saved under random names outside the code folder through the files helper. Delete the file if the database write fails. Show upload progress (NFR-PF-03).
- Every submit and every decision is kept as a row in inspection_events (Proposal), including return remarks (FR-IN-08).
- The manager gets an in-app notification on submit (Table 3.1, no e-mail).

## Data
Migration: checklist_items, inspections, inspection_results, inspection_photos, inspection_events. Seed the 24 items. Partial unique index on inspections(vehicle_id) for open statuses. Checks for remarks and for one result per item.

## API
GET /api/inspections?status=. GET /api/inspections/:id. POST /api/inspections. PUT /api/inspections/:id. POST /api/inspections/:id/photos. POST /api/inspections/:id/submit.

## Screens
- Inspections queue: status filter, empty state.
- Inspection form (the most important staff screen): progress at the top (checks done, photos added); one-tap result buttons at least 44 px high; the remark box only for Fair and Poor; notes for damage and repaint; mileage; document check; photo upload with captions; Save draft; Submit with a plain list of what is missing; the manager's remarks banner for a returned inspection.
- Build the form in three steps and commit after each: the checklist, then the notes, then the photos.

## Not in this slice
Approve, return and reject (S5), the public report (S7), PDF export.

## Done when
1. A full inspection of 24 items with photos can be saved, left, reopened and submitted on a tablet-sized screen.
2. Submit with a missing item, no mileage or fewer than 4 photos is refused with a clear list.
3. The vehicle is Under Inspection from the start; the manager is notified on submit.

## Tests that must exist (AT-17 to AT-20)
- Normal: start, save draft twice (the latest answers are kept), add photos, submit.
- Refusals: start for a vehicle that is not Received. A second open inspection. Another branch. Submit with missing items, no mileage or too few photos. A text file renamed to .jpg. A 6 MB photo. Editing after submit.
- All or nothing: a forced failure while saving photos leaves no half-saved record.
